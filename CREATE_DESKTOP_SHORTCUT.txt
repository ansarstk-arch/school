  HOW TO CREATE A DESKTOP SHORTCUT (OPTIONAL - MAKES IT EASIER)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Follow these steps to create a shortcut on your desktop:

1. RIGHT-CLICK on "START_SERVER.bat"
2. Click "Send to" → "Desktop (create shortcut)"
3. A shortcut will appear on your desktop
4. (Optional) Rename it to "Start School System"

Now you can double-click the desktop icon to start everything!
